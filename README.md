# The Way of Christ

> "Ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls." — Jeremiah 6:16

A plain, Anabaptist rule of life — a Scripture-centered exploration of Christian discipleship, faith, and daily practice through the teachings and example of Jesus Christ.

This is not a new gospel but an old obedience: to live simply, plainly, and together, under the Scriptures and the Lordship of Jesus. It stands in the Anabaptist stream of the church — believers baptized upon their own confession, refusing the sword and the oath, plain and yielded, gathered out of the world.

## Reading it

The entire work is a single, self-contained web page. There is nothing to install and no build step.

- **Online:** open the published site (served from `index.html` via GitHub Pages).
- **Locally:** clone the repository and open `index.html` in any web browser.

```
git clone https://github.com/bip-river/thewayofchrist.git
cd thewayofchrist
open index.html          # or double-click the file
```

## Contents

The work is arranged as a table of contents leading into seven parts, plus a preface:

- **Preface** — what this is, and where it comes from
- **I. Foundations** — what this way is, and how it is held
- **II. The Confession** — what is believed
- **III. Peace and the Powers** — the state, the oath, and the sword
- **IV. The Rule of Life** — the standing order of our days
- **V. Practices and Ordinances** — the visible acts of the faith
- **VI. Life Together** — belonging, ministry, and restoration
- **VII. Prayers and the Order of Worship** — how we pray, and how we gather

## Features

- **Single file, no dependencies** — all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and fonts live in `index.html`, so the page works offline and travels whole when shared.
- **Scripture at hand** — tap any reference to read the passage (King James Version) without leaving the page.
- **Full-text search** across every part.
- **Cross-references** between parts, opening at the section they name.
- **Linkable parts** — each part has its own address (for example `index.html#confession`), and reloading or pressing Back returns you to where you were.
- **Continue reading** — the contents page remembers your place.
- **Adjustable text size**, and light and dark themes that follow your device.
- **Print edition** — print, or save as PDF, for a clean booklet of the whole work.
